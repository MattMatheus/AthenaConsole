# Epic: Visual Governance & Policy Management

**As a Security Admin, I want to manage RBAC and safety policies via a UI so that I can ensure compliant operations without editing raw configuration.**

## Problem Statement
Configuring governance via API is error-prone for non-developers. A visual policy editor reduces the risk of misconfiguration and provides a clear audit trail for compliance officers.

## Success Metrics
- **RBAC UI:** Visual management of roles and permissions.
- **Policy Guardrails:** Easy toggles for concurrency limits and safety profiles.
- **Audit Visibility:** A history of who changed what policy and when.
